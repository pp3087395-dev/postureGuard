RESEARCH & EVALUATION

About PostureGuard & Model Training

Submitted for BUILD WITH ML — University of Lucknow. Engineering details, training protocol, and ethical AI considerations.

01. CLINICAL PROBLEM

The Student Spine Epidemic

Students and developers sit for 8–12 hours daily, often unconsciously adopting forward head posture ("tech neck"), lumbar slouching, and lateral pelvic tilts. Chronic postural degradation causes spinal disc compression, tension headaches, and permanent thoracic kyphosis.

Traditional posture correctors require wearable hardware or cloud streaming cameras that compromise student privacy. PostureGuard demonstrates a zero-cloud, browser-native alternative that runs locally on consumer laptops.

02. MODEL ARCHITECTURE

PoseNet & Teachable Machine

PostureGuard uses Google's PoseNet backbone ported through Google Teachable Machine Pose with TensorFlow.js. PoseNet identifies 17 2D keypoints (nose, eyes, ears, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips).

  • Model Classes: "Good Posture", "Slouching", "Neck Forward", "Leaning Sideways".
  • Self-Collected Dataset: Hundreds of webcam frames captured across varied seated setups and shirt contrasts.
  • Moving-Average Filter: Predictions smoothed across 10 rolling frames to suppress high-frequency video jitter.
  • Threshold Gate: Strictly requires ≥ 80% confidence before triggering state changes.
03. LIMITATIONS & ETHICS

Responsible AI Statement

In compliance with ML evaluation standards, we explicitly acknowledge our prototype's physical and algorithmic boundaries:

  • Lighting & Contrast: Poor backlighting or loose clothing may degrade shoulder keypoint estimation.
  • Camera Angle: Calibrated for front/slight angle webcam placement (eye-to-sternum height). Extreme side profiles are out-of-distribution.
  • Upper Body Framing: Requires head, neck, and both shoulders in view.
  • Demographic Representation: Trained on limited student cohorts; diversity expansion is planned.
  • Non-Medical Tool: PostureGuard is an ergonomic habit reminder, not an orthopedic diagnostic medical device.
