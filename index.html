RESEARCH & EVALUATION

About PostureGuard & Model Training

Built for BUILD WITH ML — University of Lucknow. Engineering pipeline, transfer learning protocol, and responsible AI considerations.

01. CLINICAL PROBLEM

The Student Spine Epidemic

Students and developers sit for 8–12 hours daily, unconsciously falling into forward head posture ("tech neck"), thoracic slouching, and lateral torso tilts. Chronic postural degradation causes spinal disc compression and permanent thoracic kyphosis.

PostureGuard demonstrates a zero-cloud, browser-native alternative that executes inference directly on standard student laptops.

02. MODEL ARCHITECTURE

PoseNet & Teachable Machine

PostureGuard uses Google's MobileNet PoseNet backbone paired with Google Teachable Machine Pose on TensorFlow.js 1.3.1. PoseNet tracks 17 2D landmarks (nose, eyes, ears, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips).

  • Calibrated Classes: "Good Posture", "Slouching", "Neck Forward", "Leaning Sideways".
  • Moving-Average Filter: Predictions smoothed across 10 rolling frames to suppress high-frequency video jitter.
  • Confidence Gate: Strictly requires ≥ 80% confidence before triggering state changes.
03. LIMITATIONS & ETHICS

Responsible AI Statement

In compliance with ML evaluation standards, we explicitly acknowledge our prototype's physical and algorithmic boundaries:

  • Lighting & Contrast: Backlighting or loose clothing may degrade shoulder keypoint estimation.
  • Camera Angle: Calibrated for front/slight angle webcam placement (eye-to-sternum height).
  • Non-Medical Tool: PostureGuard is an ergonomic habit reminder, not an orthopedic diagnostic medical device.
